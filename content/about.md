# Roba Deli — about copy (source of truth)

**One-liner:** Helsinki's newest sandwich deli — handcrafted sandwiches, salads & provisions, from
lunch to late night.

**Concept:** an authentic New York–style deli on Iso Roobertinkatu ("Roba"). Hot, fresh, cheesy
sandwiches built to order — brisket with raclette scraped molten over the top, a proper Philly, a
focaccia Italian — alongside wraps, crisp salads, real-fruit smoothies and snacks. Quiet-luxury
feel, friendly prices.

**Proof points (anti-Subway):**
- Made to order at the counter, not pre-wrapped.
- Real raclette melted over the sandwich; fresh-baked rolls (craft brioche, sourdough focaccia).
- Fresh-ingredient bar; gravlax, brisket, halloumi — not commodity fillings.
- ⭐ 5.0 on Google (24 reviews). A customer literally wrote *"100% better than Subway."*

**Voice:** confident, warm, premium-but-approachable. Short sentences. Let the food and the gold do
the talking.
